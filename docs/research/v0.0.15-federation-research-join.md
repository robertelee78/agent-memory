## Agreed assumptions

A1–A14 in catalog §5 and export §13 form the shared ledger:

- A1: stable project UUID and location are identity evidence, not credentials;
  possession of either establishes no access or transfer authority.
- A2: independent membership (M), metadata visibility (D), destination read (R),
  export (X), expansion approval (E), and provider disclosure (P).
- A3: structure/metadata and bodies have distinct disclosure authority.
- A4: versioned, destination-bound, revocable, non-transitive grants.
- A5: controlled derived invalidation never deletes the source bank; already
  disclosed bytes cannot be recalled.
- A6: unavailable is not no-match; foreign failure cannot block local memory.
- A7: no unadmitted, quarantined, secret-bearing, or deleted export/discovery.
- A8: operator/source owner/destination owner/federation steward responsibilities
  stay distinct; Vince input is unavailable, not negative evidence.
- A9: deny before opening foreign banks, indexes, caches, or graphs; no denied
  project labels, counts, paths, bodies, node IDs, or distinguishing diagnostics.
  Constant-shape/latency mitigation is a future design requirement, not a proven
  timing-noninterference guarantee.
- A10: foreign graph structure needs D and content needs X; canonical banks do
  not acquire cross-project edges.
- A11: current-project default and explicit foreign destinations.
- A12: no implementation or shipment claim; #55/#57 remain unscheduled.
- A13: E receipts bind session, developer, origin/destinations, query, operation,
  policy/authority versions, expiry and bounded paging. Changes, reset, or retry
  after denial/cancellation require a fresh decision; only the same bounded
  paging/retry request may reuse a still-valid E receipt.
- A14: stale builders recheck current authority/tombstones before publication.

## Ownership and join outcome

#246 owns identity/membership, D, authorized-set interfaces, and pre-query denial.
#247 owns X/E/P, eligibility, provenance, revocation, outage, and derived erasure.
#246 decides vocabulary/identity wording when the two briefs differ.
#253 owns the trusted confirmation-channel execution design; #56 owns foreign
shared-entity graph queries; #257's local graph has no federation dependency.

The authoring agents initially reported no disagreements. Independent review
found conflicting denial/outage vocabulary and an unsupported timing guarantee.
The revised briefs resolve both: whole-request public outcomes are `available`,
`approval-required`, and `unavailable`; internal denial/staleness distinctions
stay private, and timing mitigation remains unqualified. #246 owns vocabulary;
#247 owns outage semantics. #247 additionally proposes a
source-side allowed-provider-class term in X before foreign text can be sent to
a remote provider, as well as the destination operator's separate P approval.
This is compatible with authority separation but is **not an approved product
policy**. Other proposed grant expiry, public/private direction, stewardship,
attribution, and offline-use decisions remain explicit research outputs.
