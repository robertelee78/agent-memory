## Sign-conversion mechanism vs. candidate selection — correctly distinguished

Verified at primary source: `memgraph v3.10.1 src/storage/v2/indices/vector_index.cpp:455` applies `std::abs(SimilarityFromDistance(…, result_keys[i].distance))` during **result construction after** USearch returns candidates; `vector_index_utils.hpp` `SimilarityFromDistance` returns `1.0 - distance` for `cos_k` (so signed −1 → +1). The report's distinction — conversion corrupts reported similarity/thresholding downstream but does not itself prove ANN candidate selection used absolute cosine, and rescoring cannot recover unreturned candidates — is exactly right. Consistent with the retained core diagnostic (`diagnostic-20261106-opus/report.json`: `distance: 2.0, similarity: 1.0` for `investigation/synthetic`, with the diagnostic's own `ann_score_warning`). The report properly flags that the pinned binary was **not** rebuilt from the tag; source is "a mechanism consistent with" the observation, not proof of binary identity.
