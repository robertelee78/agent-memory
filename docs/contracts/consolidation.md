# Source-bound learning consolidation

Work key: `robertelee78/agent-memory:ADR-0005:consolidation`.
The portable [guide](../../skills/agent-memory/consolidation/GUIDE.md) documents
the packaged `consolidate_learning.py` preview/apply interface. The agent judges
meaning; the helper binds that judgment to observed sources, qualified capture,
actual Git context and the complete recoverable canonical delta.

One to eight currently qualified typed equivalents retain one existing stable
ID and a higher active version. Different conditions, type, validity, trust or
visibility cannot be folded. Every source keeps its full metadata, origin,
evidence and historical body; new capture provenance remains inactive. Previous
correction/supersession and derivation links survive. Repeating an unchanged
maintenance goal produces no new event; replaying an exact operation preserves
later edits. Revoked, expired or terminally deleted provenance cannot be revived.

Conflicts and uncertainty stay with the detecting developer/session; another
developer's conflicting claim produces a recommendation to clarify, never an
automatic message. Distinct higher-order patterns remain proposals. Procedural
captures require their separate explicit promotion review.

The public copied-pack suite `tests/contracts/test_consolidation.py` exercises
duplicates with independent developer/session origins, primary selection that
differs from lexical order, new capture provenance, no-op convergence and replay,
unknown/mismatched conditions, valid-time and trust differences, contradictory
and uncertain claims, higher-order proposals, terminal source invalidation,
new arrivals, tampered deltas, changed Git/source evidence, nested indexes, and
interruption before or after the verified completion point. It preserves code,
index and HEAD in real isolated Git worlds.

Before completion, interrupted maintenance requires rollback and fresh comparison;
after a complete exact receipt, recovery only cleans the committed outcome.
Existing common receipt guards reject unknown or changed journals/receipts.
Inputs are 256 KiB, complete transaction proposals 32 MiB, group provenance at
most 64 observed references, and changed canonical records/router 16 KiB each.
Exhausted bounds refuse without dropping evidence. Whole-bank I/O still scales
with its size; this contract makes no large-bank latency claim.

This local operation grants no publication permission, latest-main freshness,
semantic truth, or cross-machine delivery. The `correction-erasure` release
suite remains pending until the separate correction/forgetting story ships.
