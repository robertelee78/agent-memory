### 5.2 Separate eligibility domains

The manifest MUST bind **three separate inventories** and per-inventory exclusion bases:

[C:§§5–5.1; A24:R3/R4]

### 7.2 No detached-worker bank-lock acquisition

**The detached graph/vector maintenance worker MUST NOT call `local_registry.exclusive` on `.agent-memory.lock`, create a canonical writer marker, or take a long-lived source barrier.** Candidate capture, full bank hashing/parsing, frontier/authority discovery, source eligibility, database computation, artifact validation and service/driver work occur with neither bank lock nor source barrier nor publication/budget/ownership lock held. The worker owns only its private namespace builder flock during a resumable ≤30-second slice. Existing native canonical writers retain their original bank-lock semantics; G3 does not steal or automatically recover their markers. A graph worker killed at any worker failpoint closes only kernel flock descriptors and can never leave a new bank marker that blocks checkpoint/writes. [T:38/40; A24:R4; C:§§9–11]

`source-sequence.slots` is a **best-effort derived coherency hint**, not a canonical-write prerequisite or source authority: `{schema:"agent-memory/semantic-source-sequence-v1",namespace_id,witness_epoch:UUID,sequence,phase:"quiescent"|"canonical-write",operation_id,bank_observation_sha256|null,frontier_digest|null}`. Healthy hooks attempt the existing two-preallocated-4096-byte-slot HMAC update before and after the native mutation while already owning the native source barrier; no new semantic lock, graph/model work or bank-size operation is added. **Ordinary non-privacy canonical writes, checkpoints (including apply_checkpoint), and private-frontier/settings updates MUST continue their native commit when the slot, token key or any semantic-v1 path is absent, corrupt, unsafe, readonly, full or otherwise unusable.** Only errors from this optional semantic helper are converted to a content-free `witness-unusable` outcome; no native identity, canonical-source, held-frontier, security, transaction or existing bank-lock error is suppressed. The helper never creates/repairs the semantic tree, key, lock or slot as a side effect of an ordinary write and never overwrites an unsafe object. It attempts to invalidate an existing owned witness and notify the started worker, but failure to persist that invalidation is not permission to fail or replay the canonical write; nor may its absence be interpreted as proof of coherence. A completed native receipt stays completed even if every semantic hint fails. [T:38/40; A24:R4 L259–261/L285; A27:D3; C:§10]

For derived work, the rule is the opposite: missing/corrupt/unsafe witness/key/tree is unusable, not an implicit zero sequence or an empty bank. The builder MUST refuse publication without an internally minted **fresh ObservationWitness** from the most recent complete native scan in its current ≤30-second slice, binding the entire bank-state key, exact source hashes, independently observed frontier/authority/profile/engine, pointer digest/epoch, protected witness epoch/counter/read-set and inhibit check. An old HMAC-valid slot alone is never that witness. A changed or unknown observed pin discards/interrupts the candidate under C §9 L966; it is never silently rebased. Reads still independently recheck **the complete current key and inhibit immediately before disclosure**, not merely that witness. Missing witness normally maps to unavailable/graph-unavailable; a proven changed key maps to stale/source-stale; actually observed higher §12.1 integrity/ownership/erasure causes retain precedence. A missing verification key is not, by itself, proof that a caller forged a reference. No qualified empty/no-match, vector send or stale guidance is emitted. [C:§§4/9/11–12.1]

**Correctness with fail-open writers and interpretation I-PIN-01:** healthy before/after hints plus the existing exclusive writer/shared final guard retain r2's seqlock detection for cooperating writers. When a hint update is lost, unchanged sequence bytes are **not** proof of unchanged sources; the full source/key checks remain the authority. The final full hash/frontier/profile check is outside publication, with its fresh protected witness/read-set/inhibit/epoch/pointer pins rechecked under publication. A missing/invalid fresh witness rejects the publication attempt. If an otherwise unobservable missed hint races a canonical commit after the full hash, the CAS can at worst point at a generation that is now **stale**: the builder's final independent current-key check cannot report a qualified current build for it, and every reader's independent complete current-key recheck rejects it before disclosure. Do not label such a pointer current, erase the observed stale outcome, or republish over a newer pointer. Source changes observed before CAS still discard as required by L966. This is the explicit proposed interpretation of C §9 L1000–1002, not a claim of an atomic bank snapshot or that a lost seqlock update remains a proof. **Contract-owner acceptance of I-PIN-01 is a Ready gate; absent acceptance the design cannot be treated as complying with that wording.** No bank-size scan is moved under the bank/publication/source locks to hide the interpretation. [C:§§4/9; T:37–40]

**Recovery does not repair or gate canonical locks.** Native ordinary writes/checkpoints keep working before and during recovery. Only explicit setup/start/rebuild, or a worker already within a still-verifiable started envelope, may re-establish owned semantic metadata after full source/frontier/authority/tombstone/epoch observation and normal ownership checks. Unsafe objects are preserved; unknown old resources/worker identities use §4.3's exact-resource reconciliation and may keep graph capability unavailable, never native writes blocked. Search/related cannot recreate keys/slots or start workers. A new protected witness epoch invalidates all old candidate witnesses even if the numeric counter restarts; interrupted candidates cannot publish on a reset counter. A valid fresh witness and a fully validated newly bound generation must be rebuilt before derived qualification resumes; if ownership/epoch cannot be proved, stay fenced. None of this requires manual canonical bank-lock recovery caused by G3. Privacy operations use the separate fail-closed path in §9.2, not this best-effort helper. [C:§§4/9–11; T:38/40]

The same source-change guard also covers cooperating **private frontier/authority mutations**, not just Markdown: native recovery/hold/identity/settings mutating entries acquire the existing source barrier exclusively **at entry before their own later locks**, and **attempt** the best-effort content-free sequence around the verified mutation; failure does not block the native non-privacy mutation. An entry currently using a shared read barrier must select exclusive mode from the start for this mutating path; it MUST NOT upgrade under completion/bank/publication locks. Pure source observations remain shared/nonblocking or optimistic as specified. This integration is part of the source-bound recovery/authority adapters, not an assumption that unchanged bank hashes prove unchanged holds. The final guard additionally compares the exact freshly observed authority/frontier header read set (bounded by the profile's ≤64-KiB aggregate metadata-guard allowance); a larger/unknown read set fails safely with unavailable/graph-unavailable for reads or pending/resource-limit for builds, never an invented complete token. The complete frontier/native validation itself remains outside shared locks. [C:§§4/9/11; T:37/40]

To avoid a stream of short shared guards starving a native writer, the existing canonical-writer barrier wrapper best-effort emits/refreshes a **content-free priority hint** before each nonblocking exclusive attempt and its existing ≤25-ms acquisition retry. The preallocated protected `write-demand.slots` uses the same two-slot length/payload/HMAC framing and contains `{schema:"agent-memory/semantic-write-demand-v1",namespace_id,operation_id,owner_pid,owner_start_identity,boot_id,until_monotonic}` with a 250-ms priority deadline. New graph shared guards check it before and immediately after acquiring SH and yield while a fresh demand is present; they never wait/retry under SH. A successful native writer clears only its own still-current hint; concurrent requests refresh theirs. This record is **not a lock, safety witness, inhibit or consent authority**: expiry only releases scheduling preference, never clears a privacy/source fence or validates source bytes. A crashed producer leaves no kernel lock or permanent canonical exclusion; ordinary writers never wait for this hint, builder or graph service. Malformed priority state makes graph work defer, not canonical writes fail. Thus once current ≤100-ms metadata guards drain, new graph readers/builders do not leapfrog the waiting canonical writer. [T:40; C:§§10–11; engineering scheduling realization]

The worker snapshots by reading exact native scope/settings/frontier/source sequence, then a complete native observation, then repeating those pins and markers. It restarts/defer on source-writer contention or any changed/incomplete observation; it never asks a canonical writer to wait for the scan. Final full source/frontier/engine/profile/authority validation also happens outside all shared locks. Immediately afterward it takes the existing source barrier **shared, nonblocking, only for the constant-size commit guard**, verifies the same quiescent source sequence/native marker absence/authority header identities, then takes publication nonblocking and verifies current inhibit/epoch/envelope/old pointer digest. All source-bearing reads repeat complete native checks before final guard/output; a sequence/stat value never replaces content hashing. Manual/noncooperating edits retain native full-content/marker/final-check refusal, not a new claim of atomic isolation from raw writers. [C:§§4/9–11; T:40]

### 7.3 Candidate and pointer protocol

1. Capture the optimistic complete snapshot above under private builder ownership. Durably journal candidate UUID/namespace/epoch/full pins/expected old pointer digest/planned resource intents before content. No source or bank lock covers the scan.
2. Allocate stopped empty resources through §4.3. After exact allocation readback/journal, build the graph outside shared locks. Copy verified unchanged rendered/profile vector keys into independent target-owned storage, fill only changed/new eligible keys when a real provider is selected, and journal completed batches. Changed profile requires a newly bound fill; full reembed is explicit, not the default checkpoint response.
3. Checkpoint on each ≤30-second slice: resolve/cancel/journal in-flight work, persist exact resume phase/artifact pins; release builder ownership between slices. Restart discovers durable journals/intents without parent-written markers. Resume only the same candidate with every original pin/epoch unchanged, otherwise discard/dispose and take a new snapshot/new UUID.
4. Validate exact expected projection, source/framing/assertion hashes, templates/plans, owner resources, exclusion inventories, required vector artifacts and all original pins. Prepare/fsync immutable manifest and validation proofs outside shared locks. Ready is not active/read authority.
5. Complete a final full optimistic native check; take only the short source shared guard and publication flock as above. CAS `active.json` on both captured pointer digest and current epoch, also checking quiescent source sequence, inhibit, approved envelope/profile/authority header versions. Atomic pointer temp is prepared/fsynced **before** locks; inside locks are bounded read/CAS/rename/directory-fsync/readback only. A loser discards/disposes, never overwrites or reuses the same attempt against a newer pointer.
6. Outside locks, reconcile state/journal/receipt and retire the previous owned generation. Readers use one immutable manifest/instance and recheck current inhibit/pointer epoch/full source key/frontier immediately before output. Exact completed retry returns historical receipt; no republish or stale-body fallback occurs.

This follows C §§4/9–11 and adds no per-source delta freshness exception. Source-complete validation is never done under the existing bank marker lock. Normal source/epoch/policy changes discard the candidate, not silently rebase it. [T:37/40]

The exact generation states/transitions are C §9's `planned`, `building-graph`, `filling-vectors`, `validating`, `ready`, `active`, `retiring`, `interrupted`, `discarded`, `erasure-pending`, `erased`. Namespace/result pending/stale are never states. Pre-active interruption stores the prior phase; changed pins/cancel/validation/CAS loss discards; retirement/discard leads only to verified erased or erasure-pending. Any non-erased generation may be privacy-fenced to erasure-pending. No state advances by age. [C:§9]

## 9. Locks, privacy, owned erasure and authenticated technical approval

### 9.1 Single lock order and exact lock contracts

The global order is **installation runtime lock (when a lifecycle operation needs it) → namespace builder lock → existing source barrier(s), sorted by canonical root → existing bank scope/write lock → namespace publication lock → installation endpoint-budget lock**. A path may skip locks; it may never acquire an earlier family while holding a later one. Multi-namespace private builder/publication operations are avoided: lifecycle enumerates/fences one namespace at a time in stable order. Existing source transport/completion locks retain their own source-family suffix; graph code does not acquire them under bank/publication/budget or call a source observer that nests them across graph work. The ownership/port lock is a **separate terminal leaf**: it may be taken alone or while only namespace builder ownership is held, never with runtime/source/bank/publication/endpoint-budget locks. No holder acquires any other family. Thus a builder can reserve a prevalidated port without dropping single-builder ownership, and releases ownership.lock before proceeding down the source→bank→publication→budget chain. Completion may use endpoint-budget alone and releases it before any other lock. This refines, not reverses, C §11's builder→bank→publication→budget order. [C:§11; existing `installation.locked`, `turn_sources.source_barrier/private_lock`, `private_state.activation_guard`]

New lock files are stable owner-only regular/single-link inodes protected against symlink/parent substitution, created once with O_EXCL initialization plus directory fsync at explicit setup. They use `flock(LOCK_EX|LOCK_NB)` (source guard uses the **existing** `LOCK_SH|LOCK_NB` barrier), never O_EXCL PID-marker lifetime ownership. Descriptor/inode/parent identity is rechecked; fd close/process exit releases ownership. Content-free diagnostic owner metadata is separate from the stable lock inode. PID/age/name never proves ownership or permits unlink/replacement of the inode. Unsafe inode replacement refuses with ownership-unverified; no “stale lockfile delete” recovery exists for the new locks. [C:§§4/9–11; engineering realization]

### 9.2 Canonical deletion/consent fence, including whole-bank purge-data

For a bank with an enrolled graph namespace or retained semantic cleanup obligation, all privacy-changing canonical operations durably persist `Inhibit={schema:"agent-memory/semantic-inhibit-v1",operation_id,namespace_id,precommit_bank_state_sha256,intended_canonical_commit_sha256,prior_epoch,reason}` while already owning native canonical scope/write authority **before** the first prohibited canonical replacement/detachment. This is private metadata only; it needs no running graph/provider. If the inhibit cannot be created/fsynced/read back (including out-of-space), the operation **aborts before canonical commit**, returns the native honest failure and leaves canonical bytes unchanged. It MUST NOT claim deletion occurred or rely on an unwritten inhibit. If failure follows actual canonical commit, retain the existing durable inhibit and derived erasure-pending; do not replay/reclassify committed source effects. [C:§11; A24:R4; T:37–38]

**Bank with no graph enrollment:** if the existing native evidence records no enrolled graph namespace and no retained enrollment/cleanup obligation for this bank, forget, consent change and whole-bank purge follow their ordinary native protocol. They MUST NOT create/write a semantic native inhibit, enrollment record or graph metadata, and G3 adds no fail-closed prerequisite for a user who never set up graph. Absence of generated `semantic-v1/` is not this condition: a retained native enrollment, known prior ownership or unresolved cleanup evidence still takes the enrolled-bank path below; unreadable required evidence is not proof of no enrollment. Setup durably enrolls the content-free bank binding under existing native authority **before** any namespace may hold source-derived content; before a first generation may publish, it MUST complete fresh current tombstone/consent and any pending native privacy reconciliation, then satisfy the normal full-observation/current-key publication checks. A forget that completed before enrollment is therefore respected by first build, not retroactively inhibited or imported from an old snapshot. ### 10.2 Deterministic reasons and field defaults

Use only safely observed applicable triggers. Status order MUST be `invalid-request → erasure-pending → refused → approval-required → failed → incompatible → unavailable → stale → withheld → clarification-required → pending → partial → qualified`; within one status use C §12.1's row order, not exception arrival or a convenient success. All qualified exits 0, all operational nonqualified exits 1, invalid-request exits 2. Status/resource/generation are independent; pending/stale never become generation states. [C:§§9/12.1]

The producer uses the **entire** C §12.1 trigger table without adding reason enums. G3's operative mapping is:

- Invalid → `invalid-request`.
- Privacy inhibit/old-epoch unresolved copies → `erasure-pending`.
- Refusal order: `foreign-scope`, `foreign-reference`, `invalid-reference`, `invalid-related-seed`, `byo-graph-erasure-unverifiable`, provider-only `over-context/unsafe-endpoint` when observed, `ownership-unverified`.
- Authorization → `approval-required`; generic operation failure before acquisition failure: `operation-failed`, then `acquisition-failed`.
- Incompatible order: G2-reported `model-drift`, `invalid-response`, then `engine-unqualified`, `vector-integrity`, `cache-integrity`, `manifest-integrity`.
- Unavailable order: disabled/unqualified/absent similarity `profile-disabled`, G2 endpoint `endpoint-unavailable`, acquisition `acquisition-offline`, required graph/native seam `graph-unavailable`, stopped recall/diagnose `runtime-stopped`.
- Stale order: `resource-set-changed`, `source-stale`, `stale-reference`, and G2-selected-map-only `missing-score`.
- Proven source-level native outcomes retain `withheld` or `clarification-required` without private replacement metadata.
- Pending order: build/maintenance stopped `runtime-stopped`, stop `termination-unverified`, `remote-budget-exhausted`, `resource-limit`, `storage-exhausted`.
- Partial order: `partial-coverage`, eligible `empty-input`, document `over-context`, G2 semantic `truncation-unverified`, `identity-unverified`.
- Qualified order: `already-running`, `already-stopped`, `completed`. `literal-fallback` is only the actual permitted native mode-switch exception.

No trigger is inferred from a null field, inactive source, missing coverage or phase never reached. Fields for safely observed secondary deficits stay in existing assessment/exclusion/native fields, not a new public reasons array. An outage cannot manufacture hidden-replacement evidence; an incomplete superseder inventory cannot prove absence. [C:§§12–12.1; S:$defs.reason_code]

Lifecycle field template: `requested_mode:lifecycle`, `effective_mode:lifecycle` only for actual inspection/effect else none; `semantic_status:not-requested`, `semantic_reason_code:null`, semantic coverage not-applicable, native coverage null, results/groups empty, selection/page null, purge null except purge operations. `source_currency` not-applicable unless actually checked; graph-only vector capability not-applicable unless vector readiness inspected. Unknown configuration/state/identity stays null/unconfigured/unknown, never a fabricated active generation. [C:§12.2; S]

Standalone related: requested related, effective related only if traversal executed else none; semantic status not-requested/coverage not-applicable, actual seed native coverage and source currency retained. Top-level selection reference is requalified seed only for execution; next_page is actual qualified continuation. Recorded-only vector capability not-applicable; similarity reports actual independent provider capability. No new similarity-capability field. All local v1 operations use remote freshness not-applicable, which does not describe embedding destination locality. [C:§12.2]

`related/search/get/explain` are read-only: mutated/resource_changed false. Mutated means actual requested durable private operational state change, not canonical mutation or observational/send-audit journalling. Resource_changed means actually observed owned allocation/transition/disposal, including effects before failure. No-op already-running/stopped flags are false unless an independently required operational change occurred. Queue count is observed or null, not invented zero. Purge only uses S's exact required object. [C:§12.2; S:$defs.purge]

Semantic coverage is complete only with complete native recovery, exact fresh source/profile/epoch, complete authorized score map, verified identity/processing and no exclusion except capped. Combine actual deficits as unavailable → stale → partial → unverified → complete; preserve native recovery counts/frontier/security/supersession independently. `missing-score` is evaluated only after authorization/availability/fresh source/inhibit qualification, emitted only with semantic stale; a later higher fence removes the now-inapplicable diagnostic. No score is owed to history/withheld/excluded sources. Identity/alias evidence is never upgraded by operation success. [C:§§8/12–12.1]

Before provider contact, long-query shortcut or fallback, local preflight validates input/authority/privacy, exact seed/reference/runtime ownership/BYO erasure delegation, current authorization, requested similarity availability and complete selected-domain native qualification. Earlier authority refusal does not authorize foreign reads to complete that list. Incomplete required checks forbid sends/fallback. Whole-request fences produce no primaries; per-source withheld/clarification omit only the affected atomic source, retain generic exclusion and prohibit fallback, but do not prevent semantic execution for other freshly qualified safe sources. If none remain, preserve that outcome without later provider checks. Mandatory-group packing clarification is after ranking, not a prerequisite for provider contact. [C:§12.1]

Fallback permission is independent of primary-reason precedence. Erasure, invalid input, missing approval, authority refusal, withheld/clarification, unavailable selected similarity (absent/unqualified/stopped) or incomplete preflight forbid it. Only explicit permitted fallback after a semantic status other than qualified/partial runs native literal recall, with literal-fallback top-level reason and original semantic cause/status/coverage retained. Semantic reason equals top-level cause absent fallback, null for semantic qualified/not-requested. Provider-only query-over-context/unsafe-endpoint does not itself forbid literal read, but every other observed fence still applies. The graph adapter MUST NOT weaken this producer contract by dropping requested exploration or returning literal output itself. [C:§12.1; T:45]
