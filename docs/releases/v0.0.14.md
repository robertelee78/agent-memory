# v0.0.14 — Consistent bank eligibility and owned-hook health

## Complete bank eligibility (#245)

`bank validate`, ordinary readers and mediated writer preconditions now share
bounded complete-bank eligibility. Every complete canonical source, including
ordinary context and frontmatter, must fit 16,384 UTF-8 bytes. Existing limits of
65,536 sources and 64 MiB per bank remain unchanged. Complete-bank rendering and
writer preview/apply check the actual intended result before replacement.
An unrelated path, ID or lexical selector cannot hide an unreadable source.

Capacity refusals retain a typed source-byte, aggregate-byte or source-count
cause with a relative path, limit, unit and truthful exact/lower-bound measure.
They do not claim otherwise valid layout, semantic admission or novelty. Ordinary
recall and new capture remain pending. Preserve bank, feature work, original
index/HEAD and recovery evidence: this runtime has no supported source-preserving
layout-recovery command. Do not truncate, delete, skip sources, raise normal
limits or initialize over an existing bank. Retrying alone is not repair.

File-only validation remains available without Git or identity setup. Known
owned multi-file transaction markers retain their exact ownership allowance,
including complete inventories larger than the small lock-record limit; unknown
markers remain refused without reading their bodies. Unsafe and changing sources
retain non-capacity refusals.

## Receipt-owned hook health (#243)

Doctor and lifecycle preflight now use the same exact owned-entry definition for
Codex and Claude hook JSON. Recorded prompt and Stop groups must each occur once
with their complete adapter/command fields unchanged. Unrelated valid settings
and hook groups may differ; diagnostics do not rewrite the file or receipt.
Missing/changed/duplicate groups, malformed or duplicate-key JSON, unsafe paths,
mode drift, insufficient legacy receipt evidence and concurrent edits fail with
bounded, non-sensitive failure codes.

This is entry-scoped ownership, not historical whole-file equality or proof of
client loading, callback execution, source coverage, capture, publication or fresh
recall. OpenCode plugin links and other managed targets retain their strict
checks. Explicit uninstall verifies its planned post-removal output separately,
preserving unrelated configuration and transaction recovery.

The unavailable originally reported bank and unknown terminology are not repaired
or captured by this release. Source-preserving layout recovery and the other
reviewed roadmap tasks remain outside v0.0.14.

Focused local qualification passed 167 tests on each platform, plus public bank,
hook and preservation scenarios. The [qualification record](../research/2026-11-01-v014-qualification.md)
identifies source-bound coverage and retained historical failures; the complete
historical matrix and v0.0.2-updater compatibility are not claimed passing.
