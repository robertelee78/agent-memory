## Fresh guard mechanics without an observation cache

Whole-bank scans can reuse an internally owned parent descriptor chain only for
siblings in one directory. Reused siblings receive fresh pre-open ancestry checks;
every leaf retains its post-read binding and complete byte checks. No observation
survives a scan boundary. Layout checks use fresh no-follow stat and reject
unexpected errors, symlinks and incomplete inventories rather than infer absence.
The [ADR clarification](../adr/0026-source-bound-sharing-performance.md#measured-guard-mechanics-clarification-2026-11-10)
declares the permission/race differences and received actual Fable and Opus review.

Leaf descriptors retain one explicit owner through buffered-stream construction.
A controlled failure reproduced an unrelated-descriptor close before the fix;
the corrected public reader refuses while preserving that descriptor and source.
Actual installed admission interruption, known recovery and historical replay
also preserve audit/canonical bytes and feature/index/HEAD. The
[source-bound scoped proof](../research/data/2026-11-10-refresh-guard-mechanics.json)
retains the source reviews and regression results. The final-runtime Linux
functional closure also passed all 25 workers (70 methods) under the unchanged
300-second bound.

A separate synthetic single-worker public refresh completed preparation in
160.34 seconds and application in 257.15 seconds, returned qualified exact-ID
